MAJE on the decks

From Bass music roots to Progressive Psytrance and Hard Techno

Experience

MAJE is a versatile DJ rooted in Barcelona's underground electronic scene. She has played major club stages such as Automata at Garage (now Detroit Club), sharing the line-up with Technimatic and Etherwood, as well as Impérial and Club Sauvage, where her sets blend techno and progressive psytrance.

She is also a regular at the city's bass-music bars, including Unplug, Absenta de Raval, Supermercat with the DnB crews Dropezone, Drum and Babes and The Protocol. Plus the Bar Recreo with ANK Records.

Her sound leans towards heavy bass music, but she reads every room, moving easily into techno, house, electro or world music to fit the night.
