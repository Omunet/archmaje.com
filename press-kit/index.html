Experience

Based in Barcelona’s underground electronic scene, Maje is a passionate and versatile DJ known for high-impact sets that move seamlessly between hard techno, drum & bass, and broader electronic styles. With years of experience on both large club stages and intimate, community-driven nights, she has built a solid reputation for reading the room, delivering focused energy, and creating dynamic, genre-crossing experiences.

Her most prominent stage appearance came at Automata, one of the key drum & bass parties in the Barcelona area, held at Garage in L’Hospitalet de Llobregat (now Detroit Club). There, She shared the line-up with headliners Technimatic and Etherwood, delivering a focused, high-impact DnB set on a large club stage.

She has also performed at clubs such as Impérial and Club Sauvage, where her sets blended drum & bass with techno, progressive, and psytrance influences for diverse electronic crowds. Alongside these bigger stages, she has played regularly at venues known for her dedicated drum & bass nights, including Unplug, Absenta de Raval (celebrated for its raw, community-driven DnB nights in the heart of the Raval), and Supermercat. These intimate settings allowed for high-energy, crowd-focused sets built around heavy drops and rolling basslines. At Bar Recreo, their sets mixed bass house, electro, and techno.

Within Barcelona’s drum & bass scene, she has worked with the crews Dropezone, Drum and Babes, and The Protocol.
